How this works, and how accurate it is

Every 4 hours, SpartanSeats saves the open-seat count for every section on SJSU's public class schedule. A model trained on 11 past semesters (2021–2026) looks at each open section right now (seats left, how fast seats have been going, the course, the time slot, and how the course filled in past terms) and estimates the chance it fills within 7 days and before classes start.

  • Chance it fills is the main number. When the model was tested on semesters it had never seen, it ranked a section that went on to fill above one that did not about 87–89% of the time.
  • The percentages were checked the same way. On fall 2026, sections given a 39% chance filled 39% of the time. On spring 2026 they were off by up to about 10 points, so read 40% as "somewhere around 30–50%."
  • Estimated fill window is rougher. How many days off these dates are has not been measured yet, so treat them as a guide, not a deadline.
  • Full means 0 open seats on the public schedule. Seats can reopen when students drop, and waitlists are not shown.
  • Independent study, supervision, and clinical sections are listed without a prediction.
Data: SJSU public class schedule and Internet Archive copies of it.
